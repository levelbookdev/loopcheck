Migrate an existing closeout spreadsheet. Recognized columns: tag_number, vendor, contact, phone, email, basis, basis_note, clock_start_date, duration_months. Tags must already exist in the project (import the equipment schedule first). Basis values are matched loosely (SC, substantial completion, startup, shipment…); anything unrecognized becomes other with the original text preserved in the note.
